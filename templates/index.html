How GramSetu Works

A transparent, AI-assisted grievance lifecycle from citizen submission to on-ground resolution.

📸

1. Capture & Speak

Upload real photo evidence, speak in Telugu/Hindi/English, and auto-detect your exact GPS village location.

🤖

2. AI Classification

Smart AI assesses category, determines safety priority, and routes the complaint to the exact nodal department.

🚨

3. Hotspot Mapping

Clustering algorithms detect recurring village problems and infrastructure failure hotspots.

🛠️

4. Rapid Resolution

Authorities inspect, update progress on live timeline, and notify the citizen upon verified resolution.
